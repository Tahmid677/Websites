Mini Biography of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian scientist and politician who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

Early Life

Kalam was born in a small town in Tamil Nadu and showed an early interest in science and mathematics.

Important Achievement

He played a key role in India's space program and was instrumental in the development of the Agni missile system.

Words That Inspire

His words continue to inspire millions of people around the world.
