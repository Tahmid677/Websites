Importance of Exercise for Kids

Regular physical activity is essential for children's growth and development. It helps build strong bones and muscles, improves cardiovascular health, and enhances mental well-being.

Tip: Encourage your child to engage in at least 60 minutes of moderate to vigorous physical activity every day.

Quick Health Tips

  • Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits and vegetables.
  • Get enough sleep each night.
  • Limit screen time and encourage outdoor play.
